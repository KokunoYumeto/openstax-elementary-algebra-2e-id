# Terminology and correction QA

Indonesian mathematical terminology was checked against field-usage sources
and the live terminology ledger. Verified source corrections are recorded in
the bundled `CORRECTIONS.csv`; no source correction was silently applied.
The complete reader QA receipt records structural, mathematical, accessibility,
asset, deterministic, and all-page visual checks. This release also binds the
447-row semantic math-reflow provenance and 2
append-only admitted terminology revisions spanning 11
unique modules, 49 semantic substitutions, and
31 enumerated candidate edit records.
